## Choosing the Right Gold Purity Test in the Dubai Gold Souq

Whether you are a wholesale bullion trader verifying a ten-kilo consignment, a jeweller inspecting incoming 21K stock, or a private buyer checking family gold in Deira, choosing the right analytical method determines how much confidence you can place in the result.

Below is an objective metallurgical comparison of the four purity testing methods encountered in Dubai.

## 1. Fire Assay (Cupellation Method) — The Reference Standard

- **Typical Accuracy:** ±0.01% to ±0.02% (±0.1 to 0.2 parts per thousand, ‰).
- **Sample Requirement:** Micro-sample (~0.25 g – 0.5 g) drilled or pin-sampled from a homogenized melt or bar.
- **Best For:** Cast gold bars, bullion transactions, melted scrap gold lots, refinery settlements and official dispute resolution.

Because Fire Assay physically separates gold from both base metals (via 1,050 °C cupellation) and silver (via nitric acid parting), it is unaffected by surface plating or alloy segregation. Every serious refiner and bullion house in the UAE settles high-value lots on Fire Assay certificates.

## 2. X-Ray Fluorescence (XRF) Spectrometry

- **Typical Accuracy:** ±0.2% to ±0.5% on clean, homogeneous, unplated surfaces.
- **Sample Requirement:** Non-destructive surface beam (penetrates roughly 5 to 20 microns).
- **Best For:** Rapid preliminary screening of finished retail jewelry where micro-sampling is not desired.

XRF is convenient for quick counter checks, but traders must remember its physical limitation: X-rays only read the skin of the metal. A heavily gold-plated piece or a bar with surface copper depletion will show a higher karat on XRF than its true core fineness.

## 3. Hydrostatic Specific Gravity (Density) Testing

- **Typical Accuracy:** Low (±1 to 2 karats depending on alloying metals).
- **Sample Requirement:** Non-destructive water-displacement weighing.
- **Best For:** Spotting hollow items or crude tungsten/brass counterfeits in solid coins and simple bars.

Density testing cannot distinguish between carefully balanced copper-silver-gold alloys or tungsten-cored bars because tungsten (`19.25 g/cm³`) nearly matches the density of pure gold (`19.32 g/cm³`). Moreover, trapped air bubbles or gemstones invalidate the reading.

## 4. Touchstone & Nitric Acid Scratch Test

- **Typical Accuracy:** Rough visual estimate (±1.5 to 3 karats).
- **Sample Requirement:** Rubbing a streak of gold onto a black lydite stone and applying acid solutions.
- **Best For:** Basic field screening of low-value items; never recommended for commercial valuation.

## Side-by-Side Summary for Dubai Gold Buyers

- **Highest Financial Certainty:** Always request an induction melt + **Fire Assay (Cupellation)** at an independent laboratory before settling on scrap lots or cast bars.
- **Finished Retail Pieces:** Use XRF for non-destructive screening, and follow up with micro-sample Fire Assay whenever batch value is high.
